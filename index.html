Our Investment & Acquisition Criteria

While we focus predominantly on light and medium manufacturing operations in Canada and the northern United States, we are sector-agnostic for businesses displaying solid historical margins and clear customer loyalty.

Fast Initial Evaluations

If your business matches these indicators, we are typically prepared to deliver an initial valuation offer within 7 to 10 days of a preliminary inquiry.

Sector

Light manufacturing, metal fabrication, tooling, machinery, custom packaging, physical security, B2B hardware, assembly.

Revenue Range

Typically $1 million to $50 million of recurring revenue, with EBITDA margins starting at 10%+ or stable cash flows.

History

Minimum of 5+ years of operational history with verifiable historical and financial records.

Workforce

Retained management, plant supervisor or skilled workforce. We prefer structured, self-operating setups.
